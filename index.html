The syllabus

The weeks

The course runs as a flipped classroom: in class we work through a page of carefully designed material. Weeks 1–4 are the networks half, weeks 5–7 the language half, and week 8 combines everything.

Every week ends with a Go nuts with your LLM exercise — a free-form, agent-powered analysis of the shared Marvel dataset, published here as a post. Weeks 9–13 are the project period.
